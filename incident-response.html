I Just Got Scammed: The 15-Minute Action Plan

Do not feel embarrassed—cybercrime syndicates exploit deep psychological pressure and AI to deceive even tech-savvy professionals. What you do in the next 15 minutes can halt unauthorized transfers, lock your credit, and preserve your identity.

🛡️

First: Stop Communicating & Take a Deep Breath

Hang up the phone or close the chat immediately. Do not confront the scammer, do not threaten them, and do not send "test transactions" to recover funds. Criminals rely on panic to keep you reactive. Follow the prioritized chronological steps below.

⏱️ Minute 0 to 5 • Emergency Kill-Switch

Immediate Quarantine & Financial Kill-Switch

Stop real-time adversary access and freeze outgoing payment channels before settlements clear.

REMOTE ACCESS

If the scammer instructed you to install software (AnyDesk, TeamViewer, QuickAssist, UltraViewer), physically disconnect your computer from the internet right now. Unplug the network cable or switch on Airplane Mode.

⚠️ Do NOT restart the machine yet: Some infostealers execute persistence scripts upon reboot. Sever the internet connection first, then uninstall the remote tool from a separate clean phone or device.
FINANCIAL DISPUTE

Dial the number printed directly on the back of your debit/credit card or the official 24/7 fraud hotline. Request an immediate hold on compromised cards and accounts.

🗣️ Mandatory Phone Script: "I am reporting an unauthorized fraudulent transaction induced under duress and social engineering deception. Please freeze my compromised card/account immediately and initiate a wire recall / FFKC stop on transaction reference #[Number]."
ACCOUNT SECURITY

From a different, clean device (such as your personal smartphone on mobile data):

  • Google Account: Navigate to myaccount.google.com/device-activity and click "Sign out on all devices".
  • Apple ID: Go to Settings > Your Name > Devices and remove unfamiliar laptops.
  • Microsoft / Outlook: Go to account.live.com > Security > "Sign out everywhere".
⏱️ Minute 5 to 10 • Asset Lockdown

Credit Bureau Lockdown & Credential Hardening

Prevent criminal syndicates from taking out fast loans or opening synthetic lines of credit in your name.

IDENTITY LOCK

A security freeze prevents identity thieves from opening new credit cards, auto loans, or mortgages in your name even if they have your SSN and birthday. Freezing your credit is 100% free by federal law and does not affect your credit score:

Experian Freeze → Equifax Freeze → TransUnion Freeze →
PASSWORDS & PASSKEYS

Scammers target your primary email first because password reset links for all your other accounts are sent there. Update your email master password immediately. Transition from SMS 2FA to FIDO2 passkeys or an authenticator app (such as Google Authenticator) to neutralize SIM swapping.

PREPAID & CRYPTO

If gift card codes were shared, contact the card issuer immediately (e.g. Apple Support at 1-800-275-2273). If the card balance has not been drained yet, issuers can freeze the remaining funds. If cryptocurrency was deposited via a Bitcoin ATM (Coinme, CoinFlip), report the destination wallet address to the ATM operator and submit a ticket immediately.

⏱️ Minute 10 to 15 • Law Enforcement

Evidence Preservation & Official Federal Reports

Document timestamps and file formal reports required by banks and insurance providers for claims.

FORENSIC EVIDENCE

Take uncropped screenshots of:

  • The scammer's phone numbers, WhatsApp profile, Skype handle, and email headers.
  • Payment confirmation screens, wire routing numbers, crypto destination hashes, or gift card barcode serials.
  • Any fake PDF notices, summons, or spoofed police documents sent to you.
OFFICIAL FILING

Banks and insurance providers typically require a formal Police Report Incident Number before processing fraud dispute claims. File an official complaint with federal cybercrime authorities:

FBI IC3 (US) → FTC Report Fraud → I4C Portal (India) → Action Fraud (UK) →
ENDPOINT REMEDIATION

If you clicked a deceptive tracking link or downloaded a suspicious executable, launch Windows Defender (or your installed endpoint security) and select "Microsoft Defender Offline Scan". Clear all browser cookies and saved passwords to eliminate session-hijacking tokens.
