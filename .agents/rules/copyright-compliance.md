# Rule 6: Copyright & Intellectual Property Protection Mandate (Zero Infringement)

1. **Mandatory Pre-Commit Copyright Audit**:
   - Before proposing any merge into `main` or uploading to remote, an audit for copyright violations and intellectual property risks MUST be conducted.
   - All written copy, guides, and analysis must be original work or derived from public government advisory sources (FTC, FBI IC3, CISA) under fair use educational awareness standards. Plagiarized text or copied proprietary articles are strictly prohibited.

2. **Asset & Media Licensing Verification**:
   - All images, icons, visual graphics, and stylesheets must be 100% royalty-free, original, or AI-generated without proprietary watermarks or copyright encumbrances.
   - Third-party copyrighted stock photos or scraping of proprietary imagery is strictly banned.

3. **Trademark & Nominative Fair Use Compliance**:
   - Mentions of third-party security software or platforms (e.g. Aura, 1Password, Malwarebytes, NordVPN, WhatsApp) must strictly comply with nominative fair use for identification and legitimate affiliate referrals.
   - Prominent trademark disclaimers must declare that all brand names belong to their respective trademark holders.

4. **DMCA Notice & Takedown Protocol**:
   - A dedicated DMCA / copyright contact route (`legal@scamshieldlab.com`) must remain active in the Terms of Service for prompt compliance and legal dispute mitigation.
