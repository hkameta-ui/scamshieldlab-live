Most commercial security tools operate as black boxes: you paste private correspondence into an input box, it disappears onto remote third-party servers, and you receive an arbitrary color rating with zero explanation.

ScamShield Lab operates on the opposite principle: Total Explainability and Zero-Storage Privacy. We believe users deserve to know exactly which heuristics fired, why a communication was flagged, and how the underlying mathematical model arrived at its verdict. Furthermore, privacy is not a checkbox—it is hardcoded directly into our client-side execution model.

🏛️ The 4-Layer Forensic Engine Architecture

When you paste a suspicious text message, email, or URL into ScamShield Lab, our in-browser neural heuristic engine dissects the communication across four distinct forensic layers:

LAYER 1 • COGNITIVE FORENSICS

Linguistic Urgency & Psychological Manipulation

Adversaries do not hack systems first; they hack human psychology. Layer 1 detects cognitive distortion patterns designed to short-circuit critical thinking:

  • Artificial Deadlines: Phrases like "within 2 hours", "immediate card cancellation", "final warning".
  • Family Distress Simulation: Grandparent emergency lures (car accident, bail, hospital, broken phone).
  • Authority Coercion: Impersonation of law enforcement, bank fraud desks, or tax authorities demanding compliance.
  • Secrecy Demands: Isolation commands ("don't tell anyone", "cannot speak on phone").
LAYER 2 • REPUTATION & ROUTING

Infrastructure, Domain & URL Telemetry

Analyzes hyperlinks and web destinations against known cybercrime hosting infrastructures and evasive redirection techniques:

  • High-Abuse TLDs: Flags throwaway top-level domains frequently weaponized by phishing kits (.top, .xyz, .icu, .buzz).
  • Masked URL Shorteners: Identifies carrier spam-filter evasion hops (bit.ly, tinyurl, ow.ly).
  • Deceptive Prefixes: Scrutinizes subdomains simulating legitimate services (e.g., chase-security-verify.top).
LAYER 3 • FINANCIAL FORENSICS

Payment Channels & Asset Extraction

Dissects the extraction mechanism demanded by the sender to determine financial irreversibility:

  • Cryptographic Wallets: RegEx validation of public Bitcoin (Legacy/SegWit) and Ethereum addresses.
  • Irreversible Consumer P2P: Demands via non-refundable services (Zelle, CashApp, Venmo, gift cards).
  • Micro-Fee Harvesting: Identifies $1 to $5 smishing lures (unpaid tolls, USPS parcel redelivery) engineered to lower victim guard.
LAYER 4 • AUTHENTICATION HARVESTING

2FA Interception & Account Takeover

Targets the most dangerous modern social engineering trick: harvesting one-time verification passcodes (OTP):

  • Accidental Code Interception: Detects the "I sent a code to your number by mistake, can you send it to me?" WhatsApp hijack trick.
  • Reverse Refund Trap: Phony subscription cancellation lures requiring you to provide an incoming SMS code.
  • MFA Bypass Lures: Requests to read back 6-digit verification numbers over SMS or phone.

📊 The Mathematical Scoring Formula (0 to 100)

Unlike opaque neural networks that output unpredictable numbers, ScamShield Lab uses a deterministic, audit-verifiable formula modeled on FTC, FBI IC3, and CISA public threat taxonomies:

🔴 CRITICAL THREAT (Score: 60 – 100 / 100)

Triggered when high-consequence fraud markers coincide (e.g. fraudulent link + authority impersonation, crypto address, or 2FA passcode harvesting). Immediate action required: Do not click, do not pay, report immediately.

🟡 MODERATE SUSPICION (Score: 30 – 59 / 100)

Communication contains elevated urgency phrasing, unfamiliar links, or unexpected recruitment hooks. Recommended action: Verify through an independent, pre-established channel before responding.

🟢 LOW RISK (Score: 5 – 29 / 100)

No known scam signatures, high-risk urgency markers, or malicious domains detected. Standard cyber hygiene applies.

The Suspicion Principle (Zero Complacency Mandate)

A common flaw in generic scam detectors is averaging: if an attacker writes a polite, grammatically perfect email that contains an irreversible cryptocurrency wallet address, a basic average would mark it as "Low Risk".

Under our Suspicion Principle, any verified critical threat indicator (such as an explicit crypto wallet address, an accidental 2FA interception hook, or toll smishing) immediately elevates the baseline score to at least 75/100. No amount of polite phrasing can dilute a lethal cyber threat indicator.
